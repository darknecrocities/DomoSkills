# DomoSkills — 90-Second Demo Recording Script & Navigation Guide

> **Target Duration**: ~1 Minute 30 Seconds to 1 Minute 45 Seconds  
> **Pacing**: Confident, conversational, developer-to-developer pace (~135 words/min)  
> **Recommended Setup**:
> - Browser window set to **1920×1080** Fullscreen (Chrome or Arc recommended)
> - URL: `http://localhost:3000` (clean, without `?demo=1`)
> - Hide browser bookmarks bar (`Cmd + Shift + B`)
> - Set mouse cursor size slightly larger in macOS System Settings for clear visibility (Optional)

## Detailed Step-by-Step Teleprompter Script

### Scene 1: The Hook & Registry Intro (0:00 – 0:18)

**Where to be**: `http://localhost:3000` at the very top of the page.

**Screen Action**:
1. Start recording with the full hero in view.
2. Slowly guide your cursor over the animated headline (*"Open skills. Smarter agents."*).
3. Move down to the hero search bar, click it, and type: `security`.

**Voiceover Script**:
> *"If you're building with AI coding assistants like Google Antigravity, Claude Code, Cursor, or Windsurf — you know their biggest superpower is having the right context and specialized skills.*
>
> *Welcome to **DomoSkills**: the open-source registry and discovery platform designed specifically for autonomous agent workflows."*

---

### Scene 2: Interactive CLI & Agent Compatibility (0:18 – 0:36)

**Where to be**: Still on the Hero, looking at the Terminal Card on the right.

**Screen Action**:
1. Glide cursor to the terminal window on the right.
2. Click the **"Interactive CLI"** tab to show live shell interaction.
3. Click back to **"Demo Preview"**.
4. Hover over the **"Copy"** button and click it to show the copy toast.

**Voiceover Script**:
> *"Instead of manually copy-pasting system prompts or writing ad-hoc scripts, DomoSkills lets you discover verified, cryptographically checked capabilities.*
>
> *Every single skill is audited for safety, integrity, and compatibility across your favorite coding agents, installable in seconds with a single npx command."*

---

### Scene 3: Curated Starter Packs & Wavy Carousel (0:36 – 0:56)

**Where to be**: Smoothly scroll down to the **"STARTER PACKS"** section.

**Screen Action**:
1. Scroll down until the Starter Packs section is centered.
2. Let the continuous `~` wavy sine wave carousel flow for 2-3 seconds.
3. Hover your cursor over **"Multi-Agent Swarm Orchestrator"** or **"Fullstack TypeScript Pro"** (notice how hovering pauses the flow).
4. Click the **"Pause Motion"** button in the top right of the section, then click it again to resume.
5. Click the **"Install Bundle"** button on one of the cards — watch the visual flying particle fly into the cart!

**Voiceover Script**:
> *"Need a full turnkey stack? Check out our Curated Starter Packs.*
>
> *Whether you're setting up a Multi-Agent Swarm with AutoGen and CrewAI, deploying high-throughput LLMOps pipelines, or fullstack TypeScript with Turbopack — each bundle is pre-tuned for zero-shot execution.*
>
> *Just hit Install Bundle, and it automatically stacks right into your developer manifest."*

---

### Scene 4: Agent Doctor Diagnostics (0:56 – 1:16)

**Where to be**: Click **"AGENT DOCTOR"** in the top navigation bar (`/doctor`).

**Screen Action**:
1. Move cursor to the top navbar and click `AGENT DOCTOR`.
2. As the page loads, slowly scroll down through the diagnostic suite:
   - Point at the system status indicators (Node engine, Git, package manager).
   - Hover over the Agent checks for Google Antigravity, Claude Code, and Cursor.
3. Click on one of the diagnostic tabs or copy the doctor CLI command.

**Voiceover Script**:
> *"Worried about environment mismatches? Head over to **Agent Doctor**.*
>
> *It performs a comprehensive health diagnostic across your developer environment — checking your Node engine, CLI dependencies, and verified config directories for Antigravity, Claude, and Cursor.*
>
> *It spots missing dependencies and permission traps before your agent ever touches your codebase."*

### Scene 5: Developer Stack Manifest & Outro (1:16 – 1:35)

**Where to be**: Click the **Cart / Stack Icon** `[ 🛒 ]` in the top right navbar.

**Screen Action**:
1. Click the cart icon in the top right navbar to open the slide-over drawer.
2. Point at the items listed in the **Active Stack**.
3. Click the **"TARGET AI AGENT STANDARD"** dropdown and select `Claude Code (.claude)` or `Universal Agent Standard`.
4. Point at the live receipt and the generated command.
5. Hover over **"CONFIRM & COMPILE"**.

**Voiceover Script**:
> *"When you're ready, open your Developer Stack Manifest.*
>
> *Select your target agent standard, review your compiled skills, and export directly into `.agent/skills/` or run the generated CLI command in your terminal.*
>
> *Take your autonomous agents from generic assistants to domain experts. Visit **DomoSkills** and supercharge your AI workflows today!"*

## 🎙️ Recording Tips for Best Quality

1. **Audio**: Use a dedicated microphone or headset mic. Speak clearly and maintain enthusiasm without rushing.
2. **Cursor Speed**: Move your mouse deliberately and smoothly. Avoid jittery movements. When you click, pause for a fraction of a second so viewers can see what you clicked.
3. **Screen Resolution**: If recording on a Retina Mac, use QuickTime Player (`Cmd + Shift + 5` $\rightarrow$ Options $\rightarrow$ High Quality) or OBS.
4. **Practice Run**: Do one quick silent run-through with the mouse to memorize the 5 steps before pressing Record!
